The offer Wider opportunities

A supercurriculum for every A-level subject

Every activity is free and online, chosen to go beyond the specification, and linked to the original source.

Six kinds of activity

Watch, listen, read, do, enter and apply: talks, lectures, podcasts, reading, competitions, and university and career preparation.

Kept current

Several pages refresh subject news each morning from public feeds. Each half term every page gets new picks and new activities.

Built with departments

Heads of department checked their lists and asked for additions: Art's podcasts, History's podcast rack, Psychology's free courses, DT's workshop projects.

The offer Pupils engage

Beyond the Classroom04
The offer Talents extended

Built from real material, at university level

The activities use primary sources, real data and national competitions rather than summaries.

Economics

Reading the MPC's own data

Students read the Bank of England's papers and vote on each interest-rate decision, with written reasons, before the real result is published.

Politics

Voting on real divisions

Students vote on real Commons divisions and compare their vote with the result. A daily Order Paper covers UK and US news.

Philosophy

Primary texts, labelled

Plato, Anselm, Aquinas and the early moderns in the original translations. Every anecdote is labelled attested, reported or legend.

Engineering

Workshop projects

3D printing, laser cutting and lathe projects with build logs, alongside revision binders for all nine OCR H404 topic areas.

French

Writing in French on today's news

Live headlines from nine French outlets, tagged to the Edexcel themes. Students write two sentences in French on each article they read.

Across subjects

National competitions

Physics and Chemistry Olympiads, the Linguistics Olympiad, Ritangle, Bebras, the RGS essay prize and the John Locke prize.
